---
title: "Another's Heartbeat"
description: 'Once full, once bright. Yet, embers remain.'
date: '2024-09-23'
categories:
  - Poems
published: true
---

Once full, once bright  
Fire’s tender, warm light  
Since a memory, flames long past  
No dancing shadows,  
Nor playful flickers  
Yet, embers remain.  
A searing heat  
Treading danger.  
I fear for,  
yet another’s  
Heartbeat.
