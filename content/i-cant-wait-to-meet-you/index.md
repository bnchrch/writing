---
title: 'I Can’t Wait to Meet You'
description: 'I miss you. And I can’t wait to meet each and every one of you.'
date: '2026-06-23'
categories:

published: true
---

An ordinary day.  
Big problems.  
Noise, rush, chase.  
Then she whispers.  
Small problems.

I can’t wait to meet you.

Big kick, first felt, in my hand  
at my heart.  
I can’t wait to meet you.

Packing, running, wristbands, push.  
I can’t wait to meet you.

A new soft and full cry.  
Welcome in, little darling.

Our eyes meet.  
A belly laugh  
behind your toothless smile.

First roll  
first crawl  
steps  
running.

I can’t wait to meet you.

Daycare tears  
new friends  
first enemies  
stories from books  
stories from you.

I can’t wait to meet you.

From our embrace  
you leap into the world  
to find  
yourself.

I wait at home,  
wondering who  
you’ll bring back.

I miss you.  
And I can’t wait to meet  
each and every one  
of you.
