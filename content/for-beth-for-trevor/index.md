We all start our lives  
Naked  
And bare  
Seeking delicate comfort for those first few  
Who stare  
Yet, lovingly back.

In our first home we each find,  
That wondrous beginning.

And In Between those beautiful grins we grow

Into knees that bruise and bend  
Hands that reach  
For toy  
For friend

Then at once  
Gleefully we leap  
Out that screened front door.

The company we keep  
Gathering that forest floor  
A second home for these new wayward beginners  
Once so blissfully adored.

Built,rebuilt,  
Moved, removed.  
Every fort, every castle  
Could be improved

Yet try as we might  
We still find our way home at night  
No moss, no squirrel, no perfect stick,  
Can quiet keep us from getting home sick

So we carry on,  
Attached to that first hearth and dear home,  
Still Roaming  
in search of something that feels known.

Till one  
arbitrary  
day the sun rises  
A little higher,  
We daringly aspire  
And jump  
an unfamiliar stream  
And find a reflection  
doe eyed  
A dream  
Staring yet,  
lovingly back.
