title: 'Imposter in the Halls of Heaven'

I am shallow  
An imperfect flicker  
dancing the halls of heaven  
A flawed imposter, let through by gods grace  
I do not know  
yet  
If your delicate candle  
Lights our path  
Or is ignition  
To my own  
cruel blaze  
Neither matter,  
I will bask  
in this warm light,  
for it is a gift,  
no matter the length.
