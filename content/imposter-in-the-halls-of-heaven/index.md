I am shallow  
An imperfect flicker  
dancing the halls of heaven  
A flawed imposter, let through by gods grace  
I do not know  
yet  
If your delicate candle  
Lights our path  
Or is ignition  
To my own  
cruel blaze  
Neither matter,  
For I will bask  
In this warm light  
For it is a gift,  
No matter the length.
