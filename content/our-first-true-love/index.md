A mothers touch,  
Rests gently,  
Strumming.

&nbsp;&nbsp;Tip-tap,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;tip-tap,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;tip tap

Universal its soft song,  
Beats.

Each others reminder,  
Of our first, true.  
Love
