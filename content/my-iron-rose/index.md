My iron rose,

Does not ask for the grace of spring.

For She knows,  
The language  
Of stormy weather.

The weight of rain.  
The bite of wind

The patience of her roots  
In soil,  
unkind.

My Iron rose

Tall, stemmed.  
in a neighbourhood of fallen petals.

Her patina,  
shines beautifully,  
through cloud laden light.

---

Happy birthday darling

As the years pass on by

I still look at you the same

I see that bright, kind, beautiful fierce girl I sat next to

Despite everything that has happened from that moment till now.

All the storms and sunny days

It seems like you haven’t changed at all

Youre still this beautiful, iron rose.

Love you always.

Happy Birthday
