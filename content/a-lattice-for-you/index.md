*For Paige*

Myself a lattice.  
Rigid, unchanged against cascades and shadows cast.  
Material, a recollection of forests, past.

Sproutling,  
Stirs gently at my base,  
Her shape yet above.

I am not sun,  
nor rain,  
But humble patchwork.

Left for you to discover.  
wind, wrap,  
explore,  
perhaps  
make me into something  
more.
